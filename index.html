Product Analytics

Applied Data Science Techniques for Actionable Consumer Insights

Addison-Wesley Data & Analytics Series · 2020

Predictive models tell you what will happen. This book is about why it happens, and what you can do about it. It combines statistics with social science to help you build sharper theories, better metrics, and experiments that lead to real behavior change, with hands-on examples in R.

  • Build testable theories of user behavior and design KPIs that actually mean something
  • Run more informative A/B tests, and go beyond them with causal inference methods such as difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, propensity scores, and uplift modeling
  • Forecast user populations and business needs with demographic projection techniques
  • A/B testing
  • Causal inference
  • Metrics & KPIs
  • Clustering
  • Uplift modeling
  • R

Demographic Techniques for Data Scientists

The tools of population science, brought into the modern data science workflow.

Springer Nature · Forthcoming, December 2026

Demographic Techniques for Data Scientists brings over 500 years of demographic insight into modern data science practice. For centuries, demographers have rigorously studied how populations evolve over time, developing sophisticated approaches to measurement, forecasting, and the statistical examination of population. These tools, deeply grounded in the interaction between population and time, offer powerful, underutilized perspectives for today’s data scientists.

  • Learn the foundational measures and models used to study population change
  • Apply demographic methods to real-world data science problems
  • Build forecasts and projections you can defend
  • Demography
  • Population projection
  • Life tables
  • Forecasting

About the author

Joanne Rodrigues is a data scientist with a background spanning mathematics, political science, and demography. Her work focuses on bringing social science thinking into data science practice.

She is the author of Product Analytics (Addison-Wesley) and the forthcoming Demographic Techniques for Data Scientists (Springer Nature).
